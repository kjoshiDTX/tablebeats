Quick start
  1. Press Start camera and point it at your table.
  2. Drag the 4 yellow corners to outline the play area.
  3. Pick a kit below, then tap the table (or click / use keys).
  4. Hit Record (1-bar count-in), play, and watch it fill the timeline.

Tip: set everything up with the screen open, then tilt the lid down. Your corners and pads are saved.

How taps are detected

The white ring follows your fingertip. That's the spot a tap will land on. Your hand can stay over a pad and tap again and again.

Tap mode starts listening when the camera starts. It learns to ignore its own sound from the speakers; headphones work best.
